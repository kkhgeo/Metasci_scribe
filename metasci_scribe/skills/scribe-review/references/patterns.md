# Frequent problems

Questions to ask while listing candidates in step 4. This is not a checklist: raise a problem only when it applies to this draft and you can quote where it occurs.

## 기여

- Does the stated aim match what the design measures?
- Does the conclusion claim more than the design allows: a cause from an association, a mechanism from a correlation, a trend from one sampling campaign, a general rule from one site, region, or period?
- Is the novelty claim broader than the prior work the draft actually reviews?
- Is the main result already known from the closest prior studies, with only the site or dataset changed?

## 논리

- Is "no effect" or "no difference" concluded from a non-significant result, without an interval that rules out a meaningful effect?
- Does a conclusion rest on a secondary, exploratory, or post hoc analysis presented as the primary one?
- Does good model fit stand in for an identified parameter or a causal effect?
- Are alternative explanations left open: confounding, seasonality, sampling or measurement artifacts?
- Does the headline result point the other way from the authors' own sensitivity or robustness analysis?
- Were several analyses run with only the favourable one foregrounded?

## 데이터

- Do the numbers in the Abstract and Conclusion match the Results, tables, and figures?
- Do percentages recompute from their counts and denominators? Do subgroup counts sum to the total?
- Does each key estimate carry its uncertainty: an interval, an error, or n?
- What is the independent unit? Repeated measurements of one site, well, or sample, and observations close in space or time, are not independent samples.
- How are values below the detection or quantification limit handled?
- Are units and bases consistent: dry or wet weight, per volume or per mass, concentration or load?
- Is something not reported rather than not done? Ask for it to be reported; do not assert it was not done.

## 문헌

- Does a load-bearing citation support the sentence it is attached to, or does the cited study address a different task, setting, or variable?
- Does the draft omit prior work that contradicts its result, or that its argument depends on?

## Traps when checking your own criticism

- A median, quartile, or interquartile range does not constrain the tails, so it cannot show that an extreme value is impossible.
- An agreement or correlation coefficient (κ, ICC, r) does not constrain the means or the distribution.
- Rounding can hide or create small differences. Compare numbers at their printed precision before calling them inconsistent.
- A method may be described in a supplement or another section. Search before calling it missing.
